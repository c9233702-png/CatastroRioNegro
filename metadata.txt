about=An advanced cadastral tool designed for the Directorate of Land Registry of Rio Negro. It features three core modules: 1) Automated vacant land (baldios) segmentation by matching valuation databases (CSV), parcel frameworks (VM1), and vector geometries. 2) Real-time topological and orthogonal correction for boundary walls and newly digitized structures. 3) Structured mass export to CAD-ready DXF files, automatically projected into official POSGAR Gauss-Kruger flat coordinate fajas. Features real-time statistical monitoring of built-up areas and building categories.
